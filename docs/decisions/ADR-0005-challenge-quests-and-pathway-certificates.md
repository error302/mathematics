# ADR-0005 — Challenges that deepen understanding

Date: 10 October 2026, Africa/Nairobi. Authority: the owner's request for analytical/logic/comprehension/pattern follow-ups, a progressive arithmetic puzzle series, correct geometry/graphs, and a designed achievement certificate. Source-of-truth v1.2.0 Section 33 governs this work.

The design uses the existing navy/gold academy identity, editorial headings, readable mathematical examples, and restrained feedback. Practice has Foundation practice, Puzzle quests and Visual lab. Within the two new paths, a strategy and prerequisite note prepare the learner; a fresh checked task follows; a saved explanation encourages reasoning; success offers another item and a harder band. A lesson button opens analytical application. Three selectable bands let the learner adjust the challenge without timed pressure or punitive locks. They are authored difficulty choices, not an adaptive mastery inference.

Arithmetic crossings are original 3×3 puzzles with six equations, a number pool, three or four blanks, and exact standard-precedence arithmetic. Rust deterministically constructs and exhaustively checks unique solutions. The hardest band guarantees addition, subtraction, multiplication and division. An incorrect valid assignment identifies a failing row/column; an invalid pool is a format problem. The primary [Arithmo Crossmath concept reference](https://www.arithmo.com/crossmath/) was consulted, without copying game levels/assets or adopting brain-benefit claims.

Four reasoning families train planning, definitions/counterexamples, translating quantities with units, and applying stated rules. Finite-pattern ambiguity is explained rather than graded as a unique guessed continuation. The answer checker does not judge a free-text proof or explanation. Logic prompts distinguish a property that holds for some objects from a universally quantified statement; do not ask whether a false universal proposition is “sometimes” true.

Visual lab shows unit-grid rectangles, affine lines and parabolas. Rust supplies dimensions, coefficients, exact sample coordinates and quadratic Bézier control. SVG applies display coordinate transforms only. Axes, ticks, ranges and alternatives are explicit; graph viewports include the origin and all samples. Native tests verify continuous quadratic interpolation against the polynomial using rational arithmetic. Future discontinuous/parametric functions need distinct domain and rendering contracts, not a reused continuous path.

New IDs use template 1.1.0, `academy-challenge-v1`, parameter policy 1 and frozen RNG v1; prior family artifacts remain replayable. New grading/help regenerate the entire artifact, rejecting altered references. Draft restoration also regenerates and checks its hash before rendering; saved answers, notes and assistance survive ordinary reloads. Missing worker results are errors, not incorrect mathematics. Explicit support is acknowledged and does not earn independent practice XP; existing unique Rust award keys and daily cap apply.

Progress shows a printable/downloadable **Certificate of Mathematical Achievement**, advanced subtitle **Pure Mathematics Pathway — Graduate & Research Preparation**. The design is permanently marked **SPECIMEN · NOT EARNED** in this prototype. Foundations, undergraduate proof/core and graduate specialization are separately scoped future credentials with genuine assessment/review/retention gates. Local XP cannot issue one. Actual issuance/verification/revocation needs the trusted server records and controls in Section 33.5; none is represented as implemented.

Acceptance evidence: native many-seed puzzle/graph tests; 24 native/browser replay-and-grade comparisons; actual wrong/hint/retry/reward and supported-answer journeys; drafts/reload; lesson handoff; geometry and accessible graph data; 320–1440 px and keyboard controls; certificate download; old regressions; warm-cache offline behavior. Human pedagogical review, learner trials, complete curriculum, trusted assessment and credential services remain separate gates.
