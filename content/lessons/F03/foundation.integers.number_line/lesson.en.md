# Signed Integers and the Number Line

## 1. The Challenge
If the morning temperature is $3^\circ\text{C}$ and it drops by $7$ degrees, why is the resulting temperature $-4^\circ\text{C}$? Furthermore, which temperature represents colder weather: $-3^\circ\text{C}$ or $-7^\circ\text{C}$?

## 2. Predict Before Calculating
Which number is greater: $-8$ or $-2$?
- Many learners initially see the digit $8$ and think it is larger.
- But on a thermometer or number line, which value is higher up or further to the right?

## 3. Mathematical Definition: The Ring of Integers $\mathbb{Z}$
The set of integers is denoted by $\mathbb{Z}$ (from German *Zahlen*, numbers):
$$\mathbb{Z} = \{\dots, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, \dots\}$$
1. **Additive Inverses:** For every natural number $n \in \mathbb{N}$, there exists a unique negative integer $-n$ such that:
$$n + (-n) = 0$$
2. **Order Relation:** For $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}$, we define $a < b$ if and only if $b - a \in \mathbb{N}^+$ (the difference $b - a$ is strictly positive).
3. **Absolute Value (Magnitude):** The function $|\cdot| : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{N}_0$ assigns to each integer its distance from zero:
$$|x| = \begin{cases} x & \text{if } x \ge 0 \\ -x & \text{if } x < 0 \end{cases}$$

## 4. Fundamental Geometric Picture: The Signed Number Line
Visualized horizontally:
$$\longleftarrow \quad -4 \quad -3 \quad -2 \quad -1 \quad 0 \quad 1 \quad 2 \quad 3 \quad 4 \quad \longrightarrow$$
- **Rightward motion:** Represents addition of positive quantities and strictly increasing values.
- **Leftward motion:** Represents subtraction or addition of negative quantities and strictly decreasing values.
- **Transitivity of Order:** If $a < b$ and $b < c$, then $a < c$.

## 5. Worked Example: Comparing Negative and Positive Integers
Compare $-5$ and $2$:
1. Locate $0$ on the number line.
2. $-5$ is located $5$ units to the left of $0$.
3. $2$ is located $2$ units to the right of $0$.
4. Since $-5$ is to the left of $2$, we write:
$$-5 < 2$$

Now compare two negative integers: $-6$ and $-1$:
- Both are to the left of $0$.
- $|-6| = 6$ units left, whereas $|-1| = 1$ unit left.
- Because $-6$ lies further to the left than $-1$:
$$-6 < -1$$

## 6. Non-Example Trap
**Mistake:** Assuming that numbers with larger absolute value are always greater.
- While $9 > 3$, it is strictly true that $-9 < -3$.
- Think of financial debt: owing $\$9$ (balance of $-\$9$) leaves you with less money than owing $\$3$ (balance of $-\$3$).

## 7. Reflection
Zero is not merely "nothing"; it is the exact center and reference origin from which positive and negative directions unfold.

## Additional learning activities (draft)

### Prerequisite check
Order positive whole numbers and recognize subtraction as moving left on the number line.

### Representation
Draw a horizontal axis with $0$ marked in the middle. Walk $4$ steps right to $+4$. Turn around and walk $6$ steps left: you cross zero at step $4$ and land at $-2$.

### Complete a partly worked problem
Compare $-7$ and $-4$: $-7$ is $\underline{\hspace{1cm}}$ units to the left of $0$, while $-4$ is $\underline{\hspace{1cm}}$ units to the left. Since $-7$ is further left, $-7$ $\underline{\hspace{1cm}}$ $-4$.
*Self-check:* $-7$ is $7$ units to the left, $-4$ is $4$ units left; $-7 < -4$.

### Independent practice
1. Order the following integers from least to greatest: $4, -3, 0, -8, 2$.
2. Explain why any negative integer is strictly less than $0$.

### Explanation
Explain why absolute value $|x|$ measures distance without direction, and how two different integers can share the exact same absolute value.

### Transfer
A submarine is at an elevation of $-120$ meters. A diver is at $-45$ meters. Who is closer to sea level ($0$ meters), and by how much?
