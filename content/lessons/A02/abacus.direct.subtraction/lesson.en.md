# Direct Subtraction on the Soroban

## 1. The Challenge
You have set the number $4$ on the units rod ($4$ lower beads touching the reckoning beam).
You want to subtract $3$.
How do you execute this physical movement?

## 2. Predict Before Practicing
Predict:
- What is $4 - 3$?
- Which finger is used to pull lower beads away from the beam?

## 3. What is Direct Subtraction?
**Direct Subtraction** occurs when the rod already has enough **active** beads touching the beam to satisfy the subtrahend directly:
- If removing lower beads: use the **index finger** to pull the required number of beads down away from the beam.
- If removing $5$: use the **index finger** to push the upper bead up away from the beam.

## 4. Worked Example: 4 - 3 = 1
1. **Set 4:** Push $4$ lower beads up with your thumb.
2. **Subtract 3:** Using your index finger, pull $3$ lower beads down away from the beam.
3. **Read:** Exactly $1$ lower bead remains touching the beam. Result: **1**.

## 5. Direct Subtraction Involving 5: 8 - 5 = 3
1. **Set 8:** Pinch $1$ upper bead ($5$) and $3$ lower beads ($3$) toward the beam.
2. **Subtract 5:** Using your index finger, push the upper bead UP away from the beam.
3. **Read:** $3$ lower beads remain touching the beam. Result: **3**.

## 6. Where Direct Subtraction Stops
Try computing $5 - 2$.
On the rod, the only active bead is the upper bead ($5$). There are **zero** active lower beads touching the beam! We cannot pull $2$ lower beads down.
*(Solving $5 - 2$ requires the $5$-Complement Subtraction Rule: $-2 = -5 + 3$, which we explore in Course A03!)*

## 7. Reflection
Direct addition and direct subtraction establish the physical bedrock of Soroban calculation: if the beads are already there, move them directly!
